02 · Response Time

The standard, not the goal.

Form received Response is due
9am – 6pm (your shift day) Ideally 15 min · Max 2 hours
After 6pm or before 9am By 10am the next morning

The auto-confirmation text and email going out to every lead promises a fast, personal response. Missing these windows breaks a commitment we've already made.

Why 15 Minutes

The willingness window.

Industry data The first 15 minutes after a lead reaches out is the window where they are most willing to engage and move forward. After that, willingness drops sharply — a lead contacted at 30 minutes is significantly less likely to convert than one contacted at 5, and by the 1–2 hour mark you're often re-earning attention you already had.

Speed isn't about being pushy — it's about meeting people in the exact moment they decided to take action.

03 · Calendly Meetings

Next-day bookings only.

Calendly is configured so meetings can only be booked starting the next day — no same-day bookings.

Each morning you'll see the day's calls already on the schedule. Take those meetings prepared and on time, with the lead's CRM record open in front of you.

04 · AWKN Team Portal

The CRM is the source of truth.

  • Form leads automatically push into the CRM. Claim the lead as you reach out — so we know who is working what.
  • Calendly bookings (10am–6pm window): until auto-push is live, manually enter the lead into the CRM before the call.
  • Outreach activity — log every call, text, email, and voicemail in the lead record before end of day.

If it's not in the CRM, it didn't happen.

05 · Notifications

Texts to the on-duty advisor.

We're building a feature that will text the on-duty admissions advisor whenever a new contact form is submitted — so you don't have to refresh the inbox or CRM to catch leads.

Coming soon. In the meantime, keep the inbox, CRM, and Slack actively monitored throughout your shift so nothing slips past 15 minutes.

06 · What "On Duty" Means

On your assigned day:

  • Monitor the shared inbox, CRM, and Slack throughout 9am–6pm
  • Respond to form leads within 15 minutes (max 2 hours) during 9am–6pm
  • Take all Calendly meetings booked for the day, prepared with the CRM open
  • Handle leads that came in overnight before 9am — response due by 10am
  • Log all outreach in the CRM before the day ends
07 · Handoffs

Clear ownership, every morning.

If a lead came in after 6pm the prior evening, the next day's advisor owns the 10am response.

Check the CRM first thing — don't assume the prior advisor handled it. If you're handing off an active conversation, leave a clear note in the lead record so the next advisor isn't starting cold.

08 · If You Can't Cover Your Day

Confirm coverage before you step away.

If you're sick, have an emergency, or need to swap a day — contact the other advisors and me as early as possible.

Do not assume coverage is handled until someone has explicitly accepted the day. A missed day with no advisor on duty is the worst-case scenario — leads sit, the SLA breaks, and we lose people who were ready to say yes.

09 · Weekly Funnel Reviews

We tighten this together.

William and I will schedule weekly team funnel reviews with the team. We'll look at lead volume, response times, conversion through each stage, and anything getting stuck.

Bring questions, friction points, and patterns you're seeing. These are working sessions — the goal is to keep tightening the workflow together.
